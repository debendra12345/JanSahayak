## ❓ Common Judge Questions & Answers

### **Q: "Is this real? Does it work with actual government APIs?"**
A: "This MVP is a proof-of-concept. The scheme data is real and sourced from official government portals. For production, we'd integrate real government APIs. The demo works completely offline to show the concept."

### **Q: "How do you make money?"**
A: "B2G model: Governments pay for integrated citizen discovery platform. B2C model: Premium features (video guidance, document scanning). Currently: Pre-revenue MVP."

### **Q: "Why not use AI instead of rules?"**
A: "For government benefits, accuracy matters more than speed. Rule-based matching is 100% transparent and explainable. Citizens can see why they're eligible, not just 'the AI says you're eligible.'"

### **Q: "Can this scale to all 500+ schemes?"**
A: "Yes. Our architecture handles unlimited schemes. Adding new schemes takes 5 minutes (just add to database). The matching algorithm runs in milliseconds even with 1000+ schemes."

### **Q: "What's the barrier to entry for government adoption?"**
A: "Integration with existing government portals (UMANG, NSP, etc). We're designed to be a discovery layer, not a replacement. Citizens find the right scheme here, then apply on the official portal."
